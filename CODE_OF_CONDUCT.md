# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

GovInnovate AI is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and
harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of background, identity,
experience, role, or level of technical expertise.

We aim to collaborate in a way that supports public-interest innovation,
responsible technology, and constructive learning.

## Expected Behavior

Contributors and community members are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully, clearly, and professionally.
- Welcome questions and help others participate effectively.
- Give and receive constructive, specific feedback.
- Focus disagreements on ideas, evidence, and project outcomes.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and the sensitivity of government or
  community data.
- Credit the work and ideas of others.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Follow project security, contribution, and licensing policies.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention
- Trolling, insulting comments, or sustained disruption
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Deliberate misuse of project systems, data, or security information
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project repositories, issues, merge requests,
reviews, discussions, events, demonstrations, and other spaces where someone is
representing the project or its community.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to a project maintainer through the
repository hosting platform. Do not use a public issue when a report contains
sensitive personal information.

Reports should include relevant context, links, screenshots, or other available
evidence. Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect confidentiality where
possible, and avoid conflicts of interest.

Project maintainers may take actions including:

1. Providing a private correction or warning.
2. Removing or editing inappropriate content.
3. Temporarily limiting participation.
4. Permanently removing a contributor from project spaces.
5. Escalating serious threats or unlawful behavior to the appropriate platform
   administrators or authorities.

Enforcement decisions will consider impact, context, intent, repeated behavior,
and the safety of affected community members.

## Attribution

This policy is based on common open-source community standards, including the
Contributor Covenant, and is adapted for the GovInnovate AI project.
